💬 句型 jùxíng · sentence patterns for weather
怎么说天气 zěnme shuō tiānqì
how to talk about weather!
今天天气怎么样？
jīntiān tiānqì zěnmeyàng?
How's the weather today?
今天是晴天。 jīntiān shì qíngtiān.
It's sunny today.
今天 ___ 度。
jīntiān ___ dù.
Today is ___ degrees.
今天二十五度。 èrshíwǔ dù.
Today is 25 degrees.
下 ___ 了！
xià ___ le !
It's ___ing!
下雨了！/ 下雪了！ xià yǔ / xià xuě le!
It's raining/snowing!
你喜欢什么季节？
nǐ xǐhuān shénme jìjié?
What season do you like?
我喜欢春天。 wǒ xǐhuān chūntiān.
I like spring.
___ 天气很 ___ 。
___ tiānqì hěn ___ .
In ___ (season) it's ___.
夏天天气很热。 xiàtiān tiānqì hěn rè.
Summer weather is very hot.
我要穿 ___。
wǒ yào chuān ___ .
I should wear ___.
很冷，我要穿毛衣。 hěn lěng, chuān máoyī.
It's cold, I'll wear a sweater.

💡 世界的季节 shìjiè de jìjié · seasons around the world
世界的季节 shìjiè de jìjié
fun facts about seasons around the world!
🌍 1. 南半球相反 nánbànqiú xiāngfǎn
北半球是夏天时，澳大利亚是冬天！
When it's summer in the north (China/USA), it's winter in Australia! 🇦🇺❄️
🌞 2. 有的地方没有四季 méi yǒu sì jì
热带国家（如新加坡、印度）一年到头都是夏天！
Tropical countries (Singapore, India) are summer all year — no winter!
❄️ 3. 中国有名的冬天 Zhōngguó de dōngtiān
中国东北很冷 — 哈尔滨 有冰雕节！
Northeast China gets very cold — Harbin has an ice sculpture festival!
🌸 4. 樱花 yīnghuā · cherry blossoms
日本的春天有很多樱花，非常漂亮！
Japan's spring has cherry blossoms — very beautiful!
🌡️ 5. 中国最热的地方 zuì rè de dìfāng
吐鲁番 夏天可以到 50 度！
Turpan in China can reach 50°C in summer! Very hot! 🥵
