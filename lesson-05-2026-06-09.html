趣味知识
💡 家的趣味知识 qùwèi zhīshi · fun home facts
家的小知识 jiā de xiǎo zhīshi
interesting facts about homes!
🏠 1. "家" — 房子里的猪 fáng zi lǐ de zhū
"家 jiā" (home) 上面 = 房屋，下面 = 猪 (豕 shǐ) — 古代有猪就是家！
The character 家 (home) shows a roof (宀) over a pig (豕) — ancient people had homes with animals!
🎨 2. 中国的红色 Zhōngguó de hóngsè
在中国，红色 hóngsè 代表幸运和喜悦！
In China, red represents luck and joy — used at weddings and New Year!
🛏️ 3. 中国的床 Zhōngguó de chuáng
北方冬天冷，有些人睡"炕 kàng"—床下面有火！
In northern China, some traditional homes have a "kang" — a heated bed with fire underneath!
🎋 4. 亚洲家具 Yàzhōu jiājù
日本家里通常没有椅子 — 大家坐在榻榻米 tà tà mǐ（草地板）上！
Japanese homes often have tatami mats instead of chairs — you sit on the floor!
